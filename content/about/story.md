akscape began as a simple idea: a calm digital library where writing takes center stage.

In a world of endless feeds, notifications, and algorithmic noise, I wanted a space that feels different—a place to think, to write, and to share ideas without distraction. No popups, no tracking, no engagement metrics. Just words, well-presented.

The name "akscape" combines "ak" (my initials) with "escape"—a quiet corner of the internet to escape to.

## What You'll Find Here

**Computer Science & AI** — Algorithms, machine learning, systems design, and the occasional deep dive into theory.

**Mathematics & Physics** — The beautiful structures underlying our universe, from topology to quantum mechanics.

**Spirituality & Philosophy** — Explorations of consciousness, meaning, and the contemplative traditions.

**Reviews** — Honest thoughts on books, films, technology, food, and places.

**Sports & Writing** — The discipline of movement and the craft of putting words together.

## The Author

I'm Arun Kumar Venkitaraman. I write code, read papers, run trails, and occasionally finish books. This site is my digital garden—always growing, never finished.
